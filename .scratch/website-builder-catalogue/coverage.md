## Baseline and release routing

The three baseline task tickets partition assessment into forms/access, composition/publishing and delivery/quality. They gather evidence for decisions, not wholesale implementation. Every domain also routes to phased release/evidence planning through its relevant policy dependencies. Recommended and Later labels remain in the checklist until an explicit release decision changes them.
