## Question

What upload, access and cleanup contract should private form attachments have, distinct from public website Media?

## Boundaries and expected evidence

Decide allowed types/limits, authorization, temporary upload lifetime, successful-submit binding, abandoned/rejected upload cleanup, download/export access, retention and threat handling. Do not reuse the raster/PDF public Media boundary as automatic authority for arbitrary private attachments.

Coverage: FORM-03/05; SUBMIT; ACCESS-06/07; MEDIA; SEC; OPS. Acceptance remains in the [implementation checklist](../../../docs/planning/feature-implementation-checklist.md).
